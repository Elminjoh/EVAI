AI CHATBOT (ADD-ON SERVICE)
================================================================================

What is the ElmiViceAI chatbot?
-------------------------------
The ElmiViceAI chatbot is an AI assistant that sits on a business's website and chats with customers automatically, 24 hours a day. It is offered as an add-on to ElmiViceAI's main web development service, and it can be added to almost any website — whether or not ElmiViceAI built the site.


What can the chatbot do?
------------------------
  • Answer customer questions instantly, in natural, human-like language
  • Take bookings, and handle cancellations and rescheduling, with real-time availability
  • Capture enquiries and customer details for follow-up
  • Hand over to the business owner when a question needs a human
  • Take payments through Stripe integration
  • Work across multiple channels — website, SMS, WhatsApp and email
  • Provide analytics on conversations, bookings and what customers are asking


Can I see the chatbot in action?
--------------------------------
Yes. The chat assistant on the ElmiViceAI website is a live demo of the same chatbot ElmiViceAI builds for clients. You can also request a personalised demo for your business through the contact form.


Do I need a website built by ElmiViceAI to get a chatbot?
---------------------------------------------------------
No. The chatbot works on almost any website, including WordPress, Shopify, Squarespace, Wix and custom-built sites. If ElmiViceAI is building your new website, the chatbot can be built in from day one so everything works together.


How is the chatbot set up?
--------------------------
ElmiViceAI does everything for you:
  1. A quick chat about your business — services, opening hours, booking rules and common customer questions
  2. Elmi builds and trains your chatbot with your business information and brands it to match your website
  3. Elmi installs it on your website and tests it before it goes live
  4. Ongoing support — updates whenever your services, prices or hours change

There's nothing technical for you to do.


Can the chatbot match my branding?
----------------------------------
Yes. The chat widget is customised with your colours and logo so it feels like a natural part of your website.

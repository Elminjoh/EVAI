AI CHATBOT (ADD-ON SERVICE)
================================================================================

What does the AI chatbot do?
----------------------------
The AI chatbot sits on your website and handles customer conversations automatically. It can:
  • Answer customer questions 24/7
  • Handle bookings, cancellations and rescheduling
  • Process payments via Stripe integration
  • Escalate to you when a question needs a human
  • Work across multiple channels (website, SMS, WhatsApp, email)


Do I need a website built by you to use the chatbot?
----------------------------------------------------
No. The chatbot works on almost any website — WordPress, Shopify, Squarespace, Wix, custom-built sites and more. If I'm building your website, I can build the chatbot in from day one.


How do I get the chatbot set up?
--------------------------------
I do it for you. We have a quick chat about your business, I configure and brand the chatbot, install it on your website and test it before it goes live. There's nothing technical for you to do.


Can I customise the chatbot's appearance?
-----------------------------------------
Yes. The chat widget is branded with your colours and logo to match your website.


Is there analytics for the chatbot?
-----------------------------------
Yes. You can see conversations, booking metrics and customer insights so you know how the chatbot is performing.


Can I try the chatbot before buying?
------------------------------------
Yes — the chat bubble on the ElmiViceAI website is a live demo of the same chatbot I build for clients. You can also get in touch to arrange a personalised demo for your business.

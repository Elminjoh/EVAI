FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does web development pricing work? +

Every web project is quoted individually based on scope and complexity. We start with a free discovery call, then I send a detailed proposal with a fixed price. No hourly billing surprises.

What's included in a website project? +

Design, development, mobile responsiveness, basic SEO setup and launch are all included, along with post-launch support. Extras such as e-commerce, booking systems or custom features are scoped and priced up front in your proposal.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance? +

Yes. Post-launch support is included with every project, and I can also provide ongoing maintenance for updates, content changes and fixes. Get in touch to discuss what you need.

Can I add a chatbot to a website you didn't build? +

Yes. The AI chatbot works on almost any website, including WordPress, Shopify, Squarespace, Wix and custom-built sites. I handle the installation for you.

What does the chatbot setup fee cover? +

The one-time £250 setup fee covers building and configuring your chatbot — training it on your business, setting up booking rules, branding it and installing it on your website. After that, it's £125 per month.

Can I cancel the chatbot subscription? +

Yes. There are no long-term contracts — you can cancel at any time with no penalties. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing cycle.

Hi, I'm Elmi

I'm a full-stack developer and the person behind ElmiViceAI. I started it after seeing how many small businesses were losing customers online — outdated websites, slow pages, and enquiries that went unanswered for days.

Today I design and build modern, responsive websites and web applications for businesses that want a professional online presence without the agency price tag. For clients who want to go further, I also build AI chatbots that answer questions and take bookings around the clock.

I run ElmiViceAI on my own, so when you work with me you deal directly with the person designing and building your site — no account managers and no hand-offs. I'm also available for freelance and contract work with agencies and development teams.
